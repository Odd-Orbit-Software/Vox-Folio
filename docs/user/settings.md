# Settings

Settings are kept on the TV, per profile. OK steps to the next value. On rows with a range of values, Left and Right step down and up.

## Playback

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Book speed, Podcast speed ★ | 0.75× to 2×. A change applies at once if that kind is playing |
| Skip back, Skip forward | 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds |
| Smart rewind | Replay a few seconds when you resume after a pause |
| Sleep timer | What the Yellow button starts with |
| Sleep fade | Short, medium or long: 10, 30 or 60 seconds |
| Autoplay | Play what's next in a series, playlist or podcast |

## Home rows

Show or hide each Home row. With a row focused, Channel up and down move it. **Reset Home rows** puts them back as they started.

## Screen

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Screensaver | **Moving cover** shows the time and your book drifting slowly. **Screen off** is a black screen, kindest to an OLED panel. Any key wakes either one |
| Night mode ★ | A dimmer, slower screensaver from 10pm to 6am |
| Text size | **Large** makes details, hints, labels and titles bigger |
| Reduce motion | No sliding, fading or scrolling titles. The screensaver moves once a minute instead of drifting |

The screensaver appears when the TV would start its own. After an hour on it with nothing playing, Vox Folio lets the TV's power saving take over.
