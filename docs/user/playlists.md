# Playlists

Playlists come from Audiobookshelf. Make them in the Audiobookshelf web app. Book playlists are under **Library → Collections**, and podcast playlists are on the **Podcasts** tab.

A playlist's page lists everything in order, numbered. **Play all** starts at the beginning. Once you're part way through, the button reads **Continue · <title>**, and when everything is finished, **Play again from the start**. OK on any entry plays from there.

While a playlist plays:

- The top of the player shows its name and where you are, such as "Weekend queue · 2 of 3".
- Up Next follows the playlist and skips anything you've already finished.
- The **2 of 3** chip opens the playlist. From there you can jump to any entry, choose **Stop after this one**, or **Leave playlist** (it asks for a second OK). For a minute after leaving, the chip offers **Rejoin playlist**.
- Red's panel has a **Playlist** tab too.

Vox Folio remembers the playlist when you restart it. Playing one of its entries from Home keeps it going, and playing something else leaves it, with a note to say so.
